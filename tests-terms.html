Practice Tests Terms

Version v1 · November 2026

TORCH is a free, student-led volunteer program. These terms explain what happens when a parent or guardian registers a child for TORCH Practice Tests. By ticking the consent box, you confirm you are the child’s parent or guardian and agree to these terms on your child’s behalf.

What we store

  • About your child: first name, last initial, grade, school, a username, a PIN (stored scrambled, so we can’t read it), and their test answers and scores.
  • About you: your name, your email, and the date you agreed to these terms.
  • We never ask for your child’s email, birthday, address or photo.

Who can see it

  • Your child sees their own tests, answers and scores.
  • You see your children’s results through your private parent link.
  • TORCH volunteers and the head coach for your child’s school see results to help coach students and pick teams.
  • We don’t sell or share this information with anyone else, and we don’t show ads.

How answers are graded

Most questions are graded automatically. Written answers are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI, an AI service run by Cloudflare (which also hosts TORCH’s servers), to suggest a score and short feedback. Only the question, the answer key and your child’s answer text are sent — never their name. A volunteer can review and change any AI score.

Your parent link

Your parent link is like a password. Keep it private. You can make a new one at any time from your parent page, which turns the old one off. If you lose it, ask your TORCH coach for a new one.

Removing your child’s data

Email scienceolympiadqvms@gmail.com and we’ll delete your child’s account and results.

Changes

If these terms change, we’ll post the new version here and ask parents to agree again.
