Courses Offered

Science Olympiad event preparation taught by experienced high school volunteers.

Science Olympiad Events

Division B and Division C event preparation for invitationals, regionals, and state

🧬

Anatomy & Physiology

Study of human body systems including skeletal, muscular, cardiovascular, and nervous systems. Covers organ identification, system functions, and process-based questions.

Instructor: Anvitha Gottipati
Life Science
⭐

Astronomy

Stellar evolution, cosmology, deep sky objects, and the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram. Topics include spectroscopy, celestial coordinates, and solar system dynamics.

Instructor: Jairam Susarla
Earth & Space
🧪

Chemistry

Fundamental and applied chemistry including reaction types, stoichiometry, periodic trends, and lab techniques. Emphasis on problem-solving and data interpretation.

Instructor: Jairam Susarla
Physical Science
🔎

Crime Busters

Identify unknown powders,ite liquids, and metals using physical and chemical tests. Covers chromatography, flame tests, pH indicators, density, and crime scene analysis techniques.

Instructor: Adhya Kidiyoor
Life Science
🦠

Genetics (Heredity)

Mendelian and non-Mendelian inheritance, DNA structure and replication, gene expression, pedigree analysis, and biotechnology applications.

Instructor: Adhya Kidiyoor
Life Science
💧

Water Quality

Aquatic ecology, water chemistry testing, and environmental analysis. Covers pH, dissolved oxygen, turbidity, macroinvertebrate identification, and water treatment.

Instructor: Veda Khande
Earth Science
🍴

Food Science

Chemistry and biology of food including macronutrients, food safety, fermentation, browning reactions, and sensory evaluation techniques.

Instructor: Veda Khande
Life Science
⛰

Rocks & Minerals

Identification of rocks, minerals, and fossils. Covers crystal systems, Mohs hardness, streak testing, mineral formation, and the rock cycle.

Instructor: Vibha Chunduri
Earth Science
🌏

Solar System

Study of the solar system including planets, moons, asteroids, comets, and dwarf planets. Covers orbital mechanics, planetary features, space missions, and formation of the solar system.

Instructor: Vibha Chunduri
Earth & Space
🔬

Experimental Design

Design, conduct, and analyze a hands-on experiment within a time limit. Covers hypothesis formation, variables, controls, data collection, and scientific writing.

Instructor: Anvitha Gottipati
Process Skills
🔑

Code Busters

Cryptanalysis and decoding: Caesar ciphers, Vigenère ciphers, Baconian, RSA basics, and pattern recognition. Timed practice with competition-format problems.

Instructors: Shruthi Ramkumar & Aditya Jetti
Process Skills
🌐

WGYN (We've Got Your Number)

Math-focused estimation and calculation event covering scientific notation, unit conversions, Fermi questions, and order-of-magnitude reasoning.

Instructor: Shruthi Ramkumar
Math / Process Skills
💻

CAD Design

Computer-aided design fundamentals using free CAD software. Covers 3D modeling, orthographic projections, dimensioning, and engineering drawing interpretation.

Instructor: Aditya Jetti
Technology
🦠

Disease Detectives

Introduction to epidemiology: outbreak investigation, disease transmission, study design, and biostatistics. Practice interpreting real-world data sets and case studies.

Instructor: Akshara Malepati
Life Science
🚗

Scrambler

Engineering build event focused on force and motion. Design, build, and test a vehicle that uses energy transfer to travel a distance and stop at a target point.

Instructor: Akshara Malepati
Engineering
