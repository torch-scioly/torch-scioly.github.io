Tryout FAQ

When are tryouts?

Sept 3 & Sept 4, 2026, after school at Quail Valley Middle School.

Can I bring binders or cheat sheets?

No. No binders, cheat sheets, or outside reference materials allowed.

Can I share the tryout papers?

No. Do not share tryout papers — the same tests are used both days.

How are tryout events determined?

Based on the interest selections from your interest form.

Do I need to attend both days?

No — attend one day. Same tests both days.
