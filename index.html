Our Mission

Building the bridge between curiosity and achievement

TORCH is a student-led volunteer program dedicated to preparing Quail Valley Middle School students for Science Olympiad invitationals, regionals, and state competitions. Our high school volunteers bring firsthand competition experience, passion for STEM, and a commitment to making competitive science accessible to every student who wants to learn.

Tryout FAQ

When are tryouts?

September 3 and September 4, 2026, after school at Quail Valley Middle School.

Can I bring binders or cheat sheets?

No. Binders, cheat sheets, and outside reference materials are not allowed during tryout tests.

Can I share the tryout papers?

No. Do not share tryout papers with anyone. The same tests will be administered on both days.

How are tryout events determined?

Tryouts will be based on the interest selections you made on the interest form. You will be tested on the events you selected.

Do I need to attend both days?

You only need to attend one day. The same tryout tests are conducted on both September 3 and September 4.
